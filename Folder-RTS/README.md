RTS – Reality, Time, Self

A Journey Through the Layers of Consciousness and Reality

If VG is the architecture of the Network, then RTS is the exploration of lived experience within it. This series does not ask only what reality is, but also what it means to be present within it.

RTS is not a separate physical theory. It is a natural extension of VG, descending from equations into experience. It investigates how consciousness (the Self) navigates through the slices of reality, and how time itself becomes elastic when viewed through the lens of the Network.

What you will find in this series:

· Reality Slices (C_\alpha) – how the Network can sustain multiple layers of existence, each with its own emergent laws.
· The Elasticity of Time – how time ceases to be a straight line and becomes a local property that can be dilated, compressed, or reversed.
· The Self as a Nodal Entity (C_{self}) – how consciousness can interact with the Network, modulating couplings and influencing reality.
· Interfaces and Topological Gates – how certain regions of the Network become permeable, allowing passage between reality slices.
· Nodal Re-anchoring – the mechanism by which a nodal configuration can change its location in the Network, without traversing physical space.

This series is about understanding that we are not merely observers of the Network – we are active participants within it. RTS is an invitation to see reality not as a fixed landscape, but as a living territory that we can explore and, to some extent, shape.
